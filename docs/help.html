RecallOS — Help

Last updated 10 November 2026

RecallOS keeps business cards, receipts and warranties on your phone and nowhere else. That is why most answers below start in the app itself: there is no account to log into and no copy on a server to fall back on.

For a full walkthrough of every feature, see the user manual.

A scan read something wrong

The photograph is always kept, so nothing on the paper is lost even when the reading is poor. To fix a value, open the card and tap the value:

  • Wrong label (a company read as an address) — change what it is under What is this?
  • Wrong text — pick the right words under Or take it from the card; they are the text the phone read, so nothing needs typing. Or type it.
  • Missing — Add something it missed, at the end of the list.

Every value carries a small label saying where it came from: on the card (read from the photograph), you confirmed or you typed, calculated (worked out by RecallOS), or needs a look.

Cards that did not read cleanly are listed under Settings → Needs attention, with Try reading them again.

Bangla text. The phone's text reader reads Latin script (English letters and numbers). A name printed only in Bangla is usually not read. Write it in the card's note, or type it into the name — search finds both.

A card that could not be read at all is still saved. Write why you kept it in the note and you will find it by that.

Adding something without the camera

On the home screen, tap the button beside Scan:

  • Type it in — for a paper you no longer have, or that never existed: an online order, a warranty you were told about. Say what it is and what it is called, then add the rest on its own screen.
  • From a file — a PDF, JPG or PNG on your phone, up to 25 MB. It is read like a scan. Only the first page of a PDF is kept, and a PDF with a password cannot be opened.

Receipts, warranties and totals

Why is a receipt not in the total?

A receipt is added only when you have checked its total and its date, because a wrong reading would make a wrong sum. Open Receipt totals: every receipt that is not added is listed with what to do (Check the total, Add the date). A receipt that looks like a second copy of one already counted is asked about instead of being added twice. Each currency has its own total; nothing is converted.

The figure is what your saved, checked receipts add up to — not everything you spent. A refund slip comes off the figure, is marked Refund in the list, and the figure says how much came back.

When does a warranty end?

Open the warranty. Covered until shows the last covered day and how it is known: printed on the warranty, confirmed by you, or calculated from the purchase date and the length of cover. A calculated end needs the purchase date: link the warranty to its receipt (Is this its receipt? → Yes, link them, or Link its receipt), or add the date yourself. RecallOS reminds you about a month before the end.

RecallOS records what the papers say. It cannot tell you whether a shop or maker will accept a claim.

Sending papers to a shop or service centre

On a receipt or warranty, tap the share button at the top. RecallOS first shows everything that will go — every value with where it came from, and the photographs of that paper and the one linked to it — and sends nothing until you tap Share. The photographs are not encrypted once shared.

In Receipt totals, the share button sends a spreadsheet of the receipts behind the figure and those held back, each with why.

A reminder did not arrive

  1. Notifications must be on for RecallOS. When they are off, Today says so with a way to turn them on, and so does a warranty's Covered until. Next steps still show on Today either way.
  2. Reminders come back after the phone restarts, once you have unlocked it. Some phones stop apps they think are idle; if reminders still go missing, allow RecallOS to start by itself and run in the background in your phone's settings.
  3. Travelling. Days and times follow the phone's clock: a 9 AM reminder stays at 9 AM where you are, once you have opened RecallOS there. If you have not opened it, the reminder comes at the moment it would have at home.
  4. The free version keeps up to five next steps with reminders at a time. Warranty reminders are not counted in the five.

Backing up, and moving to a new phone

RecallOS keeps your wallet only on this phone, and Android's own backup is switched off for it on purpose. Uninstalling the app, or losing the phone, loses the wallet unless you made a backup.

  1. Settings → Back up the wallet.
  2. Choose a passphrase — a short sentence is easiest. Nobody can reset it, not even RecallOS. Write it down somewhere safe.
  3. Save the file somewhere that is not this phone: your email, Google Drive, a computer.

On the new phone: install RecallOS, then Settings → Restore from a backup, pick the file and type the passphrase. You see what the backup holds before anything changes; the app restarts to finish. Restoring replaces what is on that phone.

A forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered, and a backup cannot be opened without it. Take a copy in Settings makes a readable, unencrypted archive (contacts as .vcf, everything else as a file) that needs no passphrase — keep it somewhere private.

RecallOS Plus: buying, restoring, refunds

  • Plus is a single purchase through Google Play, shown at Play's own price for your country.
  • On a new phone, sign in to the Play Store with the same Google account; Plus comes back on its own. It is not part of your backup.
  • Refunds go through Google Play (your Play order history). If Plus is refunded, the app returns to the free features. Nothing in your wallet is deleted.
  • Payment problems, or a purchase that did not unlock: write to the address below with the version from Settings → Version.

Deleting, and getting things back

Deleting a card, receipt or warranty moves it to Recently deleted (on the Needs attention screen) for 30 days, where it can be put back. Delete for good removes it and its photographs permanently.

To remove everything, uninstall RecallOS. Without a backup this cannot be undone.

Contact

Settings → Send feedback opens your email with the app's version and your phone's model filled in; you see it all before it is sent. Please do not attach photographs of other people's cards.
