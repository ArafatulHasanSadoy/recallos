RecallOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

RecallOS is a business-card wallet that runs entirely on your phone. You photograph a card, the app reads it, and you find the person later by what you needed them for.

RecallOS collects no data. Nothing you put into it is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go: there is no RecallOS server, no account, and no company receiving anything.

This is enforced, not just promised

The version of RecallOS published on Google Play is built without the Android INTERNET permission. An app without that permission cannot open a network connection at all — the operating system refuses. This is not a setting you have to trust us about; it is a property of the installed app that Android itself enforces.

What is stored, and where

Everything stays in the app's own private storage on your device:

  • The photographs you take of cards, front and back.
  • Names, job titles, company names, phone numbers, email addresses, websites and postal addresses read from those cards or corrected by you.
  • The raw text the on-device reader extracted from each card.
  • Notes you write about why a card mattered.

Card details are stored in a database encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). The key is generated on your phone and held in the Android Keystore; it never leaves the device and is not known to anyone, including us. The card photographs themselves are not encrypted — they are ordinary image files in the app's private storage, which no other app can read.

Android's automatic backup is switched off for RecallOS, so your wallet is not copied to Google Drive or transferred to a new phone.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph a card. Photographs are processed on the device and saved only to the app's private storage.
  • Biometrics / device lock — only if you turn on the optional lock. Android performs the check and returns yes or no; no fingerprint or face data reaches the app.

RecallOS does not read your contacts, your location, your microphone, your files, or the other apps installed on your phone.

Text recognition and on-device intelligence

Reading a card uses Google ML Kit's text recognition, which runs on your device with a model included in the app — no image or text is uploaded. On some recent phones, RecallOS can also use the phone's own built-in language model through Android's system services; that also runs on the device. RecallOS works fully without it.

The document-scanner screen is provided by Google Play services, which may download its scanner component the first time you use it. That is a Google Play services function governed by Google's own privacy policy; no card data of yours is involved in it.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you deliberately send it, and only where you send it:

  • Sharing a contact — RecallOS writes a .vcf contact file and hands it to Android's share sheet or your contacts app. You choose the destination.
  • Taking a copy of your wallet — the export in Settings builds one archive containing your cards, contacts, notes and photographs and hands it to the share sheet. That archive is not encrypted, so store it somewhere you trust.
  • Tapping a phone number, email address, website or address — this opens your dialler, mail app, browser, WhatsApp or maps app with that value, which then handles it under its own privacy policy.

RecallOS itself sends nothing in any of these cases; it hands a value to the app you picked.

What RecallOS does not have

  • No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking.
  • No crash reporting.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party SDK that receives your data.

Other people's information

Business cards carry other people's personal details, and you are the one holding them. RecallOS keeps that information on your device only and never shares it on your behalf. Deleting a card removes it from your wallet; the "Recently deleted" screen is where a deletion becomes permanent.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling RecallOS deletes everything: the database, the encryption key held in the Android Keystore, and the card photographs. There is no server copy, so there is nothing to request the deletion of and nobody to request it from. Because deletion is total and irreversible, take a copy first if you want to keep anything — Settings → Take a copy.

Children

RecallOS is a professional contact tool intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version of RecallOS ever collects or transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
